Members of the Human- and Society-Centered AI Research Network, grouped by research line.

The network brings together psychologists, social scientists, philosophers, economists, engineers, computer scientists, mathematicians and educators. Members keep their home faculties and institutes and connect through shared questions, methods and studies.

Each dot is a member, each numbered circle a research line. Hover or focus to highlight connections, click a member to jump to their card, click a research line to open it. The same information is listed below.
